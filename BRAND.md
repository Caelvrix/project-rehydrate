# Caelvrix

**Pronunciation:** KAYL-vriks

Caelvrix is an independent technical publishing identity for practical, reusable knowledge across AI-assisted IT, infrastructure, data, security, software, governance, and technical operations.

## Purpose

Caelvrix publishes the kinds of technical guides, patterns, lessons, and open tools we wish had existed when we were the ones searching for help.

## Principles

- Practical over flashy.
- Reproducible over theoretical.
- Real failure modes over sanitized success stories.
- Clear boundaries between verified fact, inference, and opinion.
- Human judgment remains part of the system.
- Public material must be sanitized and safe to share.

## Current public project

- **Project Rehydrate** — a continuity protocol for long-running AI-assisted IT and technical work.

## Naming note

Caelvrix is the public publishing identity. Internal project names, employer systems, and private technical environments are separate and are not part of the public Caelvrix brand.

## Status

Early-stage public identity. The name should be treated as a project/publishing brand unless and until separate legal registration or trademark work is completed.
